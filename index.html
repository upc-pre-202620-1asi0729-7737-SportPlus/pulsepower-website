How PulsePower Works

Understand your body through a connected experience. From setting your goals to tracking your progress, PulsePower turns your sleep, strain and recovery data into personalized guidance for your daily routine.

01

Set Your Goals

Create your profile and tell us about your training, sleep habits and wellbeing goals. PulsePower uses this information to personalize your experience.

02

Connect Your Wearable

Link your compatible wristband to sync your physiological data. Bring your sleep, strain and recovery records together in one place.

03

Understand Your Body

Explore your daily indicators through clear summaries. Ask the AI assistant to explain your data and help you understand your patterns.

04

Plan Your Daily Routine

Receive personalized suggestions based on your recent data and goals. Use these insights to review your training intensity, rest periods and sleep habits.

05

Track Your Progress

Review your trends and progress reports over time. Recognize changes in your habits and adjust your routine as your needs evolve.

PulsePower: Understand Your Body, Support Your Wellbeing

PulsePower is a physiological intelligence platform that interprets data from a connected wearable. It brings together sleep, strain and recovery information to offer clear explanations and personalized recommendations. Built for regular exercisers and people seeking better rest, it helps users understand their patterns and make everyday wellbeing decisions based on their history and goals.

Integrated monitoring: review your sleep, strain and recovery records in a single platform.
Personalized guidance: connect your indicators to your goals and explore your data with an AI assistant.
Habit tracking: review trends and reports to understand how your activity and rest evolve.
