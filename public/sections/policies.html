Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At PulsePower we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users' personal and physiological data. This policy describes how we collect, process and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use PulsePower, we may collect:

  • Profile information: name, email address, age, weight and training or wellbeing goals.
  • Wristband data: heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), sleep duration and phases, and daily effort levels synchronized from compatible devices.
  • Interaction records: questions asked to the artificial intelligence assistant and feedback about your activities.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

The information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Generate summaries, charts and trends about your sleep, strain and recovery.
  • Provide guidance on rest and training intensity adapted to your goals.
  • Improve the platform's models and user experience.

3. Confidentiality and Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and secure storage. We do not sell, rent or transfer your personal physiological data to third parties for advertising or commercial purposes without your explicit consent.

4. User Rights (ARCO)

You have the right to access, rectify, cancel or object to the processing of your personal data at any time. You can also request the complete deletion of your account and physiological history by writing to our team.

5. Privacy Contact

If you have questions about our privacy and data protection practices, contact us at: privacy@pulsepower.io.
